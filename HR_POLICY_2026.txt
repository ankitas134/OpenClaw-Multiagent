===================================================================
ZENITH LABS PVT LTD - HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY (FY 2026)
===================================================================

1. LEAVE & VACATION POLICY:
- Paid Time Off (PTO): 24 days per calendar year (accrued 2 days per month).
- Sick Leave: 12 days annually (no carryover).
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks paid leave for female employees.
- Paternity Leave: 4 weeks paid leave for male employees.
- Work From Home (WFH): Up to 2 days per week with prior manager approval.

2. TRAVEL & EXPENSE ALLOWANCE:
- Daily Meal Allowance: ₹1,500 per day for domestic official travel.
- Flight Class: Economy class for flights under 6 hours; Business class allowed for flights exceeding 6 hours.
- Hotel Budget Cap: Up to ₹5,000 per night for tier-1 cities (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR).

3. PERFORMANCE & APPRAISAL CYCLE:
- Appraisal Review: Bi-annual (mid-year review in July, annual review in January).
- Performance Bonus: Distributed in February based on individual KPI scoring and company EBITDA margins.

4. HEALTH & WELLNESS BENEFITS:
- Health Insurance Coverage: Comprehensive floater cover up to ₹5,00,000 for employee + spouse + 2 children.
- Gym/Wellness Reimbursement: Up to ₹2,000 per month.
